CREAMY PASTA

Prep time: 45 min

INGRIDIENTS

  • Pasta
  • Cherry tomatomes
  • Heavy cream
  • Fresh Basil
  • Garlic
  • onions
  • milk
  • Butter
  • grated cheese
  • olive oil

PREPARATION

  1. Boil the penne pasta for 10 min on medium heat
  2. Finely dice your red onions and garlic also halve your cherry tomatoes
  3. Heat the olive oil and butter in a pan and then saute the onion and garlic until golden brown and then add your cherry tomatoes
  4. Pour heavy cream & milk into the pan stir it unit it thickens
  1. Add some pasta water into your pan and stir it until the texture becomes smooth
  2. Lastly add your boiled pasta into the pan and garnish it with fresh basil and grated cheese
